About Me

Cards, curiosity, and too many books on the nightstand.

Hi! I’m Gia Gupta, a Grade 12 IB student at Bombay International School, Mumbai. For as long as I can remember, I’ve been drawn to games that make you think — cards, puzzles, sudoku or the NYT games — and eventually, bridge.

Bridge is a game built entirely on incomplete information, partnership, and pressure, and it made me fascinated with how people make decisions when they don’t have all the answers. It opened my eyes to how strategy connects to almost everything, from economics and technology to human behaviour. I’m passionate about exploring those intersections, and equally passionate about making them accessible — whether that’s bringing strategic thinking into classrooms, teaching digital literacy to underserved communities, or building technology that reduces uncertainty for people who need it most.

Off the table, you’ll probably find me curled up with a fiction book, attempting new songs on my electric guitar, or getting far too competitive about tennis & pickleball.
