Percepta

An AI-powered wearable pin that delivers real-time object, colour, and hazard detection for visually impaired individuals.

The Problem & The Solution

Over 2.2 billion people worldwide live with some form of vision impairment. While screen readers and navigation apps have made digital content more accessible, real-time environmental awareness — knowing what’s around you, right now — remains a gap.

Percepta is a magnetic chest pin that uses a YOLO-based computer vision system to deliver real-time object, colour, and hazard detection directly to the wearer. I conceptualised and built the prototype, achieving sub-200ms detection latency, before pitching it to Lenskart co-founders Peyush Bansal and Amit Chaudhary. Their R&D team adopted the project, and together we’ve reduced production cost by 75% and latency by 25%.

The device is currently undergoing user testing with NGOs, and a patent filing is in process.
