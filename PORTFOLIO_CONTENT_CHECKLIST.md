## 🏠 HOME PAGE — What to Add

### Your Profile Photo
- **What it is**: A nice photo of you (head and shoulders)
- **Size**: Should be a clear, professional-looking photo
- **Format**: JPG or PNG file
- **Where it goes**: Next to the "About Me" section on the home page

### One-Line Tagline
- **What it is**: One sentence that describes YOU
- **Examples**:
  - "Student, bridge champion, and research enthusiast"
  - "Connecting bridge, research, and innovation"
  - "Bridging the gap between game theory and real-world impact"
- **Length**: Just 1-2 sentences max
- **Where it goes**: At the top of the "About Me" section

### About Me Paragraph
- **What it is**: A short introduction about yourself
- **What to include**:
  - What you study or do
  - Your main interests (bridge, research, etc.)
  - What makes you unique
- **Length**: 2-3 paragraphs (not too long)
- **Tone**: Write it like you're talking to a friend
- **Where it goes**: Under the tagline in the "About Me" section
